Frequently asked questions

What I am asked most before the first consultation: costs, insurance, hours and how to book. If your question is not here, write to me on WhatsApp.

About the consultation

What does the consultation cost?

The assessment consultation costs $800 MXN. If surgery is indicated, you receive a written quote before anything is scheduled.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes. Patients with Mexican and international policies are seen, and you receive the documentation your insurer requires. Every policy is different: check with yours whether it works by direct billing or by reimbursement.

Where is the office?

At Healthcare by the Sea, De Los Tules 168-10, Jardines de Las Gaviotas, 48328 Puerto Vallarta, Jalisco.

What are the office hours?

By appointment, Monday to Friday from 2:00 to 8:00 p.m. You can see the free slots and book yours online.

How do I book an appointment?

Online, at drjasonvaldivia.com/citas: it shows the slots that are really free in the office calendar, and your place is held immediately. You can also write by WhatsApp.

How soon can I be seen?

It depends on that week's calendar. The booking page shows the available slots for the next few days, so you can choose the one that suits you best.

Do you handle emergencies?

The office works by appointment and is not an emergency service. If you have severe pain, bleeding, high fever or difficulty breathing, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.

Do you speak English?

Yes. The consultation can be held in English, and documentation can be provided in English if your insurer requires it.

What is laparoscopic surgery?

It is a technique that allows surgery through small incisions, guided by a camera. Whether your case is suitable for this approach is decided at the assessment, based on your tests and your history.

Do I need a referral from another doctor?

No. You can book directly. If another doctor referred you, bring the summary or the tests they gave you.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Official ID, your insurance card if you have one, any related previous tests and a list of the medications you take.

Which procedures do you perform?

Gallbladder surgery, hernias (inguinal, umbilical and incisional), anti-reflux and hiatal hernia surgery, appendectomy, thyroid surgery, and cysts, lipomas and soft-tissue tumours.

A note worth reading

This page answers practical questions; it does not replace an assessment. Any decision about your case —including whether or not to operate— is made at the consultation, with your tests and history in front of us.

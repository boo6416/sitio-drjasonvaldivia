For visitors and residents from abroad

Puerto Vallarta receives patients who live here part of the year and travellers who need surgery while in town. The consultation is in English, the written instructions are in English, and you deal directly with the surgeon — not with an intermediary or an agency.

If your case is urgent, message +52 33 3971 8620 directly. Emergency surgery — appendicitis, acute gallbladder, obstructed hernia — is attended at the hospitals listed below.

Hospitals

Dr. Valdivia operates at hospitals in Puerto Vallarta, including Hospital Joya, Hospital CMQ and Clínica Angeles. Which hospital is used depends on the procedure, on your insurance and on availability; it is agreed with you at the consultation.

Insurance and cost

Major medical insurance is accepted, as well as private payment. Coverage depends on your policy, so it is reviewed with you before scheduling. The initial consultation is $800 MXN; the surgical quote is given at that consultation, once the case is known. See accepted insurance.

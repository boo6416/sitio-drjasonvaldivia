Care with health insurance

If you have a policy, you can be seen here. I provide the documentation your insurer asks for and support you through the process, from the assessment to discharge.

First, the important part: every policy is different. Before your surgery, confirm with your insurer whether your plan works by direct billing or by reimbursement, and which deductible and co-insurance apply to you. I am glad to provide the medical information they need to answer you.

How it works, step by step

  1. Assessment. Your case is reviewed and it is decided whether surgery is indicated. You leave this consultation with a written diagnosis.
  2. Documentation for your insurer. Medical report, diagnosis with ICD-10 code, surgical plan and quote. This is what your insurer needs to make its decision.
  3. Scheduling. Once authorised, the surgery is scheduled at the hospital and coordinated with your insurer.
  4. Follow-up. Progress and discharge notes, which are also part of the file your policy may request.

What to bring to your first consultation

  • Official ID and your insurance card or policy number.
  • Any related previous tests, if you have them (ultrasound, CT scan, lab work).
  • A list of the medications you take, including over-the-counter ones.

No insurance?

Self-pay patients are also seen. The assessment consultation costs $800 MXN and, if surgery is indicated, you receive a written quote before anything is scheduled. No surprises. See what surgery costs in Puerto Vallarta.

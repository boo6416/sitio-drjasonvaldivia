Credentials and hospitals of Dr. Jason Valdivia

Before having surgery with someone, you should be able to check who they are. This page brings together what can be verified: specialty, board certification and where he operates.

Specialty and certification

  • General Surgeon. Dr. Jason Alejandro Valdivia Nájar.
  • Certified by the Mexican Board of General Surgery (Consejo Mexicano de Cirugía General).
  • Advanced training in laparoscopic surgery.
  • Care in English and Spanish.
Laparoscopy is not a specialty, it is a technique. It is mentioned here as what it is —advanced training within General Surgery— and not as if it were a separate degree. When a website presents it as a specialty, it is worth asking about board certification.

How to check any doctor yourself

You don't have to take a website's word for it. In Mexico every professional licence (cédula profesional) is public and can be checked free of charge in the SEP National Registry of Professionals: cedulaprofesional.sep.gob.mx, searching by first and last names. Do it with any surgeon you are considering — this one included.
